UI design of the NEW SESSION sheet of "Shed", a macOS app for AI coding agents: a centered modal (760×560) of dark glass with a 1px hairline, over the dimmed dithered beam background.
Title "New session" with a close ×. Left column: "Computer" segmented chooser "This Mac | Jim's Mac". "Folder" with a path bar breadcrumb "~ › src › shed" and a "Filter folders" field, then a list of folders with folder icons, each with a grey "modified 2h ago" and, for git repositories, a small branch chip "main" or "feat/sites"; one row selected with a blue glass highlight. A "Recent" section with 3 recent folders.
Right column: "Agent" as three selectable glass cards with agent marks: Pi (lilac π, selected with blue border), Prime (ice sparkle), DeepSeek (periwinkle wave). "Model" dropdown open showing groups "OpenAI" (GPT-5.5, GPT-5.5 mini), "Mistral" (Devstral 2), "DeepSeek" (V4) with a checkmark on GPT-5.5. "Reasoning" segmented "Low | Medium | High". "First message" text area "Optional — what should Pi do?".
Footer: quiet "Cancel" and primary blue "Start session ⌘↩".
