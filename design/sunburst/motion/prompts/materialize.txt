UI motion reference, about 3 s, then a 1 s hold on the finished screen, looping: a phone conversation screen loading. On the near-black navy background, columns of chunky square halftone dots (a 2 pt dither grid, like a print you can see the dots of) rain down the screen like matrix code rain, but made of dots, not letters. The dots take the colours of our light beam: deep blue and electric blue near the top, cyan, a cream-white hot core at each falling column's head, then orange and red at the trailing tail fading back to dark. The rain is dense and fast for the first second, then the falling dots slow and settle into place where the conversation's content will be: they condense into the shapes of the message blocks (a right-aligned blue user bubble, a few lines of left-aligned body text, a rounded "Worked for 12s" card), dither-resolving from coarse coloured dots into crisp white text and glass cards, while the leftover rain fades out top to bottom. The composer at the bottom and the header at the top stay still the whole time. Ends on the clean, readable conversation over calm dark navy.

Style: dark UI on near-black with a fine dithered halftone dot texture, frosted-glass elements with 1px hairlines, electric blue #4C9BFF accent, beam colours from deep blue #172F80 through #3A9BFF and cyan #8ADCFF to cream #FFDB98, orange #FFA43A and red #FF3A1E, Inter-like type. Smooth, premium motion like Apple system UI. Show the phone screen large and centered so the motion is easy to read.
