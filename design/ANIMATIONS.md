# Shed — where it moves

Animation opportunities, roughly ordered by how often you'll see them. Each one should say something about what the
agents are doing; none should slow down reading or typing. Everything honours Reduce Motion (fades only).

## Always on screen

- **Agent is working** — the avatar breathes, and a faint light sweeps across the status label. When it finishes,
  one soft settle (a small bounce plus a firefly-like spark).
- **Reasoning** — the "Thinking" line shimmers like ink still drying. When the step closes, it folds into the
  "Worked for 45s" pill with a matched-geometry collapse.
- **Tool calls arriving** — rows slide up and fade in with a 30 ms stagger. The activity rail draws itself down to
  each new row, like a brush stroke.
- **Streaming text** — new words fade in per chunk rather than popping. The caret is a small glowing dot.
- **Auto-follow** — when new content arrives, the transcript glides rather than jumping. The "Jump to latest" pill
  rises with a spring and shows a count that ticks up.

## Subagents

- **Spawned** — a new avatar pops into the header stack (scale from 0.6 with overshoot); the others shift aside.
- **Working** — working avatars bob gently, out of phase, so the stack feels alive.
- **Reports in** — the report row slides out from under the subagent's avatar. Its icon flashes once in the agent's
  colour, and the avatar in the header gets a tiny check.
- **Expand a report** — the row unfolds like a scroll; the tinted rail draws downward.

## Composer

- **Send** — the bubble lifts from the composer into the transcript (matched geometry). The send button gives a
  small kick.
- **While the agent runs** — the send button morphs into Steer and Stop, the icon rotating between states.
- **Context gauge** — the ring fills with a spring as tokens grow. Past 80% it warms in colour; at compaction it
  drains and refills, with a "Compacted" toast.
- **Model / effort chips** — the effort level steps through bars (low → high) with a little bounce.

## Sidebar

- **New session** — the row grows in at the top while the list makes room. Its status dot ignites.
- **Status changes** — dot colour cross-fades; a session that finishes while you're away gets a soft unread glow.
- **Delete** — the row collapses; the remaining rows close up with a spring.
- **Selection** — a lantern-like highlight slides between rows (matched geometry), rather than snapping.

## Background commands

- **Running count** — the terminal button's number rolls (numeric text transition), and the dot pulses.
- **Command finished** — the transcript's "Background command finished" row arrives with a quick stamp (a
  hanko-style seal) in green, red or grey.
- **Cancel** — the row's progress ring winds down to a stop icon.

## New session sheet

- **Agent picker** — the selected agent card lifts and its spirit avatar waves. The other card dims.
- **Folder browser** — folders push left and right like page turns; breadcrumbs slide.
- **Start** — the sheet folds into the new sidebar row.

## Moments of delight (rare, earned)

- **Empty state** — a little spirit dozes in the shed, with drifting fireflies; it wakes when you hover New Session.
- **First message of the day** — the header moon or sun rises slightly.
- **A long run finishes** (over 5 minutes) — a burst of petals or fireflies from the status label.
- **Cost pill** — digits roll on change; a tiny coin glint each time it ticks up.
- **App launch** — the wordmark brushes itself in, and the lantern lights.
